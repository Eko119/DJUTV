About

The mic goes where the story is

DJUTV started posting in 2021 with a simple premise: Chicago’s stories are best told by the people living them, on their own block, in their own words. Five years and more than 1,500 interviews later, that premise has held.

Host DJ U works as a journalist first. He puts people on record before anyone else is asking, then follows the threads that matter to the city long after the news cycle moves on: courtrooms, families, neighborhoods, the aftermath. That is why an audience of 426,000 keeps coming back, and why the archive has been watched more than 152 million times.

No press junket. No handlers.

What we cover

  • Artists & entertainersEmerging and established, first interview or fifth.
  • EntrepreneursThe people building businesses in the city.
  • Community leadersOrganizers, families and advocates on the ground.
  • Courts & case coverageOn-the-record reporting from proceedings that shape the scene.
